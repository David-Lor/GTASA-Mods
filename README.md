# My GTA San Andreas mods

A compilation of my own GTASA mods, used in my playthrough.

- [oneShotKillPlayerMod](oneShotKillPlayerMod%5Bmem%5D.js): you die with a single gunshot. Damage from melee weapons is not altered.
- [carEngineOff](carEngineOff.js): exit your car turning off the engine, when exiting by pressing the handbrake + exit buttons at the same time.
- [enemyFixes](enemyFixes%5Bmem%5D.js): limit accuracy, max health and max armor of NPCs.
- [aimCameraFix](aimCameraFix%5Bmem%5D.js): when using controller & GInput, when aiming, makes you aim where the camera is pointing, instead of where CJ is looking.
- [energyDrain](energyDrain.js): reduce the health (a.k.a. energy at the beginning of the game) as time passes. A cheap option of having a hunger mod.
- [openAmmunation](openAmmunation%5Bmem%5D.js): unlock all the Ammunation stores early, making they enterable, so you can buy weapons before the "Doberman" mission.
- [burglaryFix](burglaryFix%5Bmem%5D.js): try to fix the house burglary side mission, which sometimes do not add the markers to enter homes. This script forces enabling the house markers during such mission.
- [armorLimitations](armorLimitations%5Bmem%5D.js): prevent the player from sprinting while wearing body armor.
- [weaponDrop](weaponDrop.ts): drop weapons, creating pickups for them. The armor can be dropped as well, but under 80% will be discarded. When saving the game, pickups generated by the mod are saved natively by the game.
- [gangwarProvocation](gangwarProvocation%5Bmem%5D.js): increases the gang war provocation threshold, so that starting a gang war is easier.
